## Practical Exercise

11. **Case Study Analysis:**
    Imagine you are a data scientist working for an e-commerce company. The company wants to understand why customers are abandoning their shopping carts before completing purchases.
    
    a) What types of data would you collect to analyze this problem?
    b) Outline a data science approach to address this issue.
    c) What AI techniques might be helpful in this scenario?

---

**Answers:**
1. B
2. C
3. B
4. B
5. B
6. Data science is a multidisciplinary field that uses various methods to extract insights from data, while AI focuses specifically on creating systems that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence. Data science may incorporate AI techniques as tools, but also includes other statistical and analytical approaches.
7. Python (programming language for data analysis), Matplotlib/Seaborn (visualization libraries), Scikit-learn (machine learning library), SQL (database query language), Tableau (visualization tool), etc.
8. Data collection (gathering relevant data), data cleaning (handling missing values, outliers), exploratory data analysis (initial investigation), data modeling (building statistical/ML models), interpretation (deriving insights), communication (presenting findings).
9. Bias and fairness in AI systems, privacy concerns with data collection, transparency and explainability of models, social and economic impacts of automation.
10. Examples: Predicting customer churn allows companies to take preventive action; forecasting inventory needs reduces costs; personalized recommendations increase sales; fraud detection prevents financial losses; predictive maintenance reduces equipment downtime.
